## TL;DR
For diamonds in modern Minecraft (1.18+): branch-mine at **Y=-59**, with tunnels 2 blocks apart, using an iron pickaxe or better. For netherite: branch-mine in the Nether at **Y=15**, with a diamond pickaxe or better, looking for ancient debris. Bring torches, food, a water bucket, and a backup plan for lava. No dupes, no exploits, no X-ray — these routes work on any survival server and will not get you banned.

## Diamonds: why Y=-59?
Since the Caves & Cliffs update (1.18), the world goes down to Y=-64 and diamond ore generates from Y=-64 to Y=16, peaking at **Y=-59**. That is the densest level, just above most lava lakes. Older guides saying Y=11 or Y=12 are from before 1.18 — ignore them on modern versions.

## Diamond branch-mining route (step by step)
1. **Gear up first:** Iron pickaxe minimum (diamond is better), iron armor, a stack of torches, food, a water bucket, and a crafting table. Enchant later — do not wait for Fortune to start.
2. **Dig down safely:** Dig a staircase, not straight down. Never dig straight down in Minecraft — it is the oldest rule for a reason.
3. **Make a main tunnel at Y=-59.** Check your Y with F3 (Java) or the coordinates display (Bedrock). Your feet should be at -59, so you are mining the -59 layer.
4. **Branch every 2 blocks:** Dig side tunnels 1 block wide, 2 blocks high, leaving 2 blocks between branches. This exposes the most ore with the least digging — diamonds spawn in veins, and this spacing catches almost every vein.
5. **Mine every ore you see, and check the walls.** When you find diamonds, mine around them carefully — lava often hides behind ore at this depth. Place water or blocks before mining the last diamond in a vein.
6. **Go home before your inventory fills.** Diamonds are useless if you die in lava on the way back. Set a waypoint, a torch trail, or coordinates for your base entrance.

**Expected rate:** Branch mining is luck-based, but a focused 30-60 minute session at Y=-59 typically yields a useful stack of diamonds for tools and armor, plus redstone, lapis and gold along the way. Do not expect a fixed number — anyone promising "64 diamonds in 10 minutes" is selling a seed or a dupe.

## Fortune vs Silk Touch: which first?
- **Fortune III** on a diamond pickaxe multiplies diamond drops — this is your farming pickaxe. Get it as soon as you can via enchanting or villager trading.
- **Silk Touch** lets you carry ore blocks home and Fortune them later, but for a first farming run, Fortune directly is simpler and faster.
- Keep a separate Silk Touch pickaxe for delicate blocks (glass, ore you want to display) — do not put Fortune and Silk Touch on the same pickaxe, they conflict.

## Netherite: ancient debris at Y=15
Netherite does not come from an Overworld ore. You mine **ancient debris** in the Nether, smelt it into netherite scrap, combine 4 scrap + 4 gold ingots into a netherite ingot, then upgrade diamond gear at a smithing table (you also need a netherite upgrade smithing template, found in bastion remnants).

Ancient debris generates from Y=8 to Y=119 in the Nether, but density peaks around **Y=15**. Mine there.

### Netherite route
1. **Gear:** Diamond pickaxe or better (iron will not drop debris), diamond armor, fire resistance potions if you have them, gold armor piece (piglin neutral), food, and plenty of blocks to bridge and wall off lava.
2. **Location:** Nether Wastes or Soul Sand Valley are good biomes. Avoid Basalt Deltas for debris hunting — debris is rarer there and the terrain is brutal.
3. **Tunnel at Y=15:** Same branch pattern as diamonds — main tunnel, side branches every 2-3 blocks. Ancient debris never spawns exposed to air, so you must dig through netherrack to find it. That is why systematic tunnels beat random wandering.
4. **Beds/TNT method (advanced, careful):** Because debris has very high blast resistance, some players use beds (which explode in the Nether) or TNT to clear large areas of netherrack quickly. This is legitimate survival gameplay, but it is dangerous: wear fire protection, keep distance, and do not use it near your portal or base. Beginners should stick to branch mining.
5. **Smelt and upgrade:** Smelt debris in a furnace/blast furnace → netherite scrap. Craft ingots (4 scrap + 4 gold). At a smithing table, combine diamond gear + netherite ingot + netherite upgrade template.

**How much do you need?** A full set of netherite armor plus tools takes roughly 16-24 ancient debris depending on what you upgrade. Farm in stages — upgrade your pickaxe first (faster mining, lava-proof items), then armor.

## Safety kit (both routes)
- Water bucket for the Overworld (lava and falls). In the Nether water does not work, so bring fire resistance potions instead.
- Torches, food, spare pickaxe, ender chest if you have one
- A written-down base coordinate, or a lodestone/compass
- Never mine the block directly under or above you without checking

## Common farming mistakes
- Mining at the wrong Y (old guides, wrong version)
- Digging straight down, or mining diamonds with a stone/wood pickaxe (they drop nothing)
- Carrying everything with no escape plan — lava, creepers and piglin brutes do not care about your diamond count
- Using X-ray texture packs or clients on servers — that is cheating, and most servers ban for it. These routes are faster in the long run because you keep your account.

## FAQ

**What is the best Y level for diamonds in 2026?**
Y=-59 on Minecraft 1.18 and later (Java and Bedrock). Before 1.18 it was Y=11-12, which is why old guides disagree.

**What is the best Y level for netherite?**
Y=15 in the Nether, for ancient debris. Debris spawns Y=8-119, but Y=15 is the peak.

**Can I mine ancient debris with an iron pickaxe?**
No. You need a diamond pickaxe or better, or the block will break without dropping.

**Is bed-mining for netherite cheating?**
No — beds exploding in the Nether is intended game mechanics, and debris surviving explosions is also intended. It is risky, but it is legitimate survival play, unlike X-ray or duping.
