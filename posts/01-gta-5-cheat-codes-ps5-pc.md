## 5 rules before you cheat

1. **Story Mode only.** Never in GTA Online.
2. **Save first.** Cheats disable Trophies/Achievements for the session. Make a clean save, then a separate "cheat fun" save.
3. **There is no official money cheat in GTA 5.** Any site offering a money code, generator or "free GTA cash" download is a scam or a ban risk — skip it. Earn in Story Mode with missions, Lester's assassination stock missions, and property income.
4. **Some missions ignore cheats.** If a code seems not to work during a mission, that is normal — try it in free roam.
5. **Restart to restore Achievements.** Quitting and reloading a pre-cheat save restores your ability to earn them.

## FAQ

**Do GTA 5 cheats work on PS5?**
Yes. The PS4 button combinations work unchanged on PS5, and the phone numbers work on every platform.

**Do cheats work in GTA Online?**
No. They are Story Mode only, and attempting to use mods or cheat tools in Online risks a ban. Do not download "Online cheat menus" — they are the fastest way to lose an account.

**Why did my cheat not work?**
Usually one of three things: you paused the game first (don't), you typed it too slowly on console, or you are in a mission that blocks it. Try again in free roam, and listen for the confirmation sound.

**Is there a GTA 5 money cheat?**
No official one exists. Ignore generators and mod menus promising unlimited money — they are scams, malware, or ban bait.
